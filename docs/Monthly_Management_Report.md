## 1. Executive summary

FY2025 revenue closed at **INR 232.8m**, ahead of the INR 225.0m budget by INR 7.8m. The revenue beat did not convert fully into cash because customers operate on 60-day terms. Cash closed at **INR 16.2m**, only modestly above the INR 12.0m minimum reserve.

The most actionable cash opportunity is receivables: total AR is **INR 36.0m**, of which **INR 17.5m is overdue**. Marketing, HR, Operations and CapEx exceeded budget, so management should combine collections action with spend discipline rather than relying only on new funding.

The FY2026 Base Case forecasts revenue of **INR 354.5m** and closing cash of **INR 84.5m**. Cash is on Watch in January–March, then moves to Healthy from April. The Worst Case falls below the reserve in January and becomes a funding event; it should trigger early contingency planning.

**Recommended decisions this month:** approve an AR collection sprint, hold the INR 12.0m reserve, stage discretionary CapEx, review Marketing and HR cost drivers, and maintain a committed contingency facility.

## 9. Management recommendations

1. **Improve receivable collections:** create a weekly list of 31+ day invoices with owner, amount, dispute reason and promise-to-pay date. Financial impact: faster cash conversion and lower borrowing requirement.
2. **Renegotiate supplier terms selectively:** target 45-day terms with strategic vendors only after discussion. Financial impact: modestly improves timing without damaging relationships.
3. **Reduce non-essential expenses:** pause low-ROI campaigns, travel and discretionary admin. Financial impact: reduces monthly burn and creates reserve headroom.
4. **Delay CapEx during Watch months:** defer purchases not linked to customer delivery, security or regulatory requirements. Financial impact: preserves cash at the point of maximum timing risk.
5. **Improve budget controls:** require monthly owner commentary for variances above 5% or a materiality threshold. Financial impact: stops recurring overruns and improves forecast accuracy.
6. **Maintain a minimum reserve:** do not treat the INR 12m threshold as spendable cash. Financial impact: protects payroll and core platform continuity.
7. **Revise assumptions monthly:** update collection rate, pipeline conversion, headcount and vendor commitments. Financial impact: reduces stale-forecast risk.
8. **Maintain a committed contingency facility:** prepare the Worst Case funding plan before it is needed. Financial impact: avoids expensive emergency borrowing and rushed decisions.
